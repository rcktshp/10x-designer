# Foundational Heuristics — Principles

The meta-principle: **reason from established, named canon and cite it by name.** When a finding maps to a famous heuristic or law, name it — it's more credible and more teachable than generic feedback ("fails Fitts's Law" beats "the button feels small").

This cluster indexes the canonical named sets so they're always available and attributable:
- **Usability heuristics** — Nielsen's 10, the 10 Content Design heuristics.
- **Interface rules** — Shneiderman's 8 Golden Rules, Tognazzini's First Principles.
- **Cognitive engineering** — Gerhardt-Powals' principles.
- **Cognitive laws** — Hick's, Fitts's, Miller's, Jakob's, Peak-End, Doherty.
- **Behavior & persuasion** — Fogg Behavior Model (B=MAT), Cialdini's principles (apply ethically).
- **Perception** — Gestalt principles of grouping.

Use alongside the domain clusters (which carry the deeper, scoreable checks). The full lists are in `heuristics.md`; scoreable checks in `critique.md`.
