**Owner:** · **Date:** · **Status:** Exploring / In design / In review / Shipped
**Links:** brief · figma · prototype

## The problem
Two or three sentences: who hurts, when, and why it matters now. Plain
language — this page travels beyond the design team.

## The idea
The solution direction in two or three sentences, leading with what changes
for the user. One falsifiable expectation if there's a metric ("should lift
completion ~X%").

## What it looks like
The single most explanatory visual — key screen, before/after pair, or flow
sketch — with a one-line caption. (Embed or link; never more than two.)

## Why this works
The 2–3 strongest reasons, each one line: a user insight, a principle, or
evidence from research/testing.

## What it costs
Scope in one line; the main risk or tradeoff in another.

## What's next
The immediate next step, who's on it, and the one thing needed from the
reader (if anything).
